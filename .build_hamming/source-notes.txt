Mathematical convention used throughout
- Even parity.
- Positions 1, 2, and 4 are check bits; positions 3, 5, 6, and 7 are message bits.
- Check 1 covers positions 1, 3, 5, 7.
- Check 2 covers positions 2, 3, 6, 7.
- Check 4 covers positions 4, 5, 6, 7.
- Syndrome address is the sum of the labels of failed checks, equivalently binary s4s2s1.
- The three-message-bit warm-up fixes position 7 to zero; it is a restricted subcode of Hamming(7,4), not the standard Hamming code.
